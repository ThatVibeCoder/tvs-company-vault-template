# <Company> Company Vault — start here

**For people and agents. Read this first.**

This vault is the company's main knowledge base. Some things live elsewhere on purpose; for those, the section points to the exact page and never copies it. It is never a log: if something gets a new row every time it happens, it lives in its own tool and this vault points at it (`architecture/Vault Architecture.md`).

## You have two repos
| Repo | What it's for | Made from |
|---|---|---|
| **This one: your company vault** (`<company>-company-vault`) | What you know, how you sound, your agent teams and their skills, and a description of everything that runs on its own. Open it in Obsidian. | github.com/ThatVibeCoder/tvs-company-vault-template |
| **Your second repo: your marketing headquarters** (`<company>-marketing-headquarters`) | The things that run: automations, GitHub Actions, the posting queue, the dashboard picture. | github.com/ThatVibeCoder/tvs-marketing-headquarters |

Later in the course your website gets a third repo of its own (`<company>-website`).
