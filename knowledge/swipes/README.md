# Swipes

**Filled by:** the Research Team lesson: its Researcher files a swipe whenever you send something that caught your eye.

A swipe is someone else's marketing that teaches a shape worth stealing: what it is, where it came from, why it works, the shape, and its job (hook, offer, proof or structure). The job tag is how a writer finds "I need a proof section" later.

One file per swipe: `<YYYY-MM-DD>-<short-name>.md` (copy `_template.md`).

**Knowledge, not a dump:** file a swipe only when it teaches a shape you don't already have. If it's the same shape as one already here, add a line to that file's Notes instead of making a new file. Keep the piece itself short (the part that does the job), and link to the original.

**What never goes here:** your own posts (they live on the platform), whole copied pages, screenshots of private messages.
