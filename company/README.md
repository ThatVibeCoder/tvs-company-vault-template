# Company

How the company itself runs: direction, people and money.

**What goes here:** `strategy/` (direction, decisions, KPIs), `team/` (who's on the team, hiring, meetings), `finances/` (income, expenses, subscriptions), `onboarding/` (a new teammate's first day).

**What never goes here:** your goals list (`knowledge/goals.md`), competitors (`knowledge/competitors/`), prices (`brand/offer.md`), a running list of every expense or every meeting (that's a log: point at your bank, accounting tool or calendar instead), keys or passwords.

**The router rule:** if you keep something somewhere else on purpose (Notion, a spreadsheet, an accounting tool), the page here points to the exact place instead of copying it.

**Example:** `finances/README.md` says "Expenses live in our accounting tool (link). Monthly tool costs are in `business.md`, What You Run On."
