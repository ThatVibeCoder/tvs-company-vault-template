# Marketing

Where you show up and how: every social platform, and notes about your websites.

**What goes here:** `social/` (one folder per platform: the account, the look, the post formats that work, the hooks that work) and `websites/` (each site you have: what it's for and where its code lives).

**What never goes here:** your posts themselves (drafts wait in your headquarters repo's `posts/` queue and leave once sent; sent posts live on the platform), your website's files (your website repo), weekly numbers (read them in your tools when you need them).

**Example:** to write an Instagram post, your AI reads `social/platforms/instagram/` and `social/hooks/instagram.md`, then `brand/voice.md`.
