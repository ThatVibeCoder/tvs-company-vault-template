# Operations

How the work gets done, every time: the how-to pages, the policies, and the rules you must follow.

**What goes here:** `playbook/` (how-to pages and step-by-step SOPs), `policies/` (what the company always does and never does), `compliance/` (rules and contracts).

**What never goes here:** the steps for a job one of your agent teams already does (those live in that team's skill; link to it instead of copying it), a record of every time a job was done (a log), keys or passwords.

**Example:** `playbook/` holds "How we answer a refund request", which ends in the reply saved as a draft. Your Dev Team's steps for shipping a page stay in its `ship-a-page` skill.
