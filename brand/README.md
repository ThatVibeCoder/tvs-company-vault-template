# Brand

Who you serve, what you sell, and how you look and sound. Every writer, page and offer reads the one file here that its job is about.

**What goes here:** `icp.md` (who you serve), `offer.md` (what you sell and its price: the price's one home), `voice.md` (a map of where your real words live), `contrarian-take.md`, `friction-points.md`, `channels.md`, `guidelines.md` (banned words and never-do calls), `colors.md`, `typography.md`, `assets/` (logos and brand images), and `icps/` and `offers/` for extra buyers and extra offers.

**What never goes here:** copies of your posts or newsletters (`voice.md` points at where they live instead), notes about one client (`clients/`), keys or passwords (the `.env` file).

**How it gets filled:** by your course lessons, through your AI. Don't fill these by hand. A file that still says `_Not filled in yet._` counts as missing, and your AI stops and asks.

**Example:** your AI is about to write a post. It reads `voice.md`, goes and reads the real words it points at, then checks `guidelines.md` for banned words.
