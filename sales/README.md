# Sales

How a stranger becomes a customer, and what you say along the way.

**What goes here:** `process/` (the stages from lead to customer), `scripts/` (call and message scripts), `psychology/` (why buyers say yes or no), `offers/` (a pointer to your offers and prices).

**What never goes here:** a running record of your leads (a file per person that grows with every touch is a log: keep leads in your CRM or Notion and point at it; see "Already here, kept on purpose" in `architecture/Vault Architecture.md`), prices (their one home is `brand/offer.md`), call recordings (they stay where your call tool keeps them).

**Example:** `process/README.md` says "Leads live in our CRM (link). A lead becomes a booked call when they pick a time." Your AI reads the CRM when it needs a lead, and never copies one here.
